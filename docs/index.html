About

I studied mathematics and statistics at UC Berkeley and earned my MD/PhD at UCLA. I completed anesthesiology residency at Brigham and Women’s Hospital, where I now practice as an attending anesthesiologist.

My doctoral work with Zhilin Qu in UCLA’s Department of Biomathematics examined how the heart’s electrical activity depends on its recent history—and how this memory can contribute to arrhythmias.

Research

I use physiological simulations and mathematical models to study how ion channels and cardiac memory contribute to electrical instability, from action potentials in individual cells to spiral waves in tissue.
